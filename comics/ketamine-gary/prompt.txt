# AGENCY™

The comic is a deadpan parody of a polished direct-to-consumer ketamine-therapy advertisement. Its central joke must never be visually announced: an ordinary man's successful mental-health testimonial gradually and inexplicably coincides with his becoming involved with Donald Trump and the machinery of presidential power. The testimonial language remains completely sincere, positive, and mundane throughout.

## STYLE

Polished contemporary **editorial-advertising comic illustration**, resembling storyboard art for a high-budget direct-to-consumer wellness campaign.

Use clean, confident ink contours; naturalistic but gently caricatured anatomy; restrained digital color; believable soft commercial lighting; crisp legibility; and smooth, clean surfaces.

**No mottling, speckling, dirty washes, distressed texture, painterly noise, or photographic realism.**

The artwork itself must remain professionally earnest. **Do not visually signal that a punchline is coming.** The increasingly absurd circumstances are depicted with the same reassuring seriousness as the wellness advertisement.
